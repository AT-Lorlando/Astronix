Passionate about science and a lifelong self-learner, I completed a cybersecurity engineering degree while running a web-development micro-business on the side (France's National Student-Entrepreneur Status). It is now my full-time activity.

A hybrid profile: I work across full-stack development, defensive security (SOC, SIEM, vulnerability management), infrastructure and DevSecOps. This triptych (Code, Security, Hardware) gives me a holistic view of modern IT systems, and above all feeds my thirst to learn and build. I'm convinced that understanding the bottom of the stack (network, OS, hardware) makes you better at every level above it.

My homelab: router, bastion, reverse proxy, internal DNS, local PKI and a Linux server with a GPU for local AI. It's my sandbox, and it lets me grow my skills by simulating a professional environment. It's also where my [personal projects](/#projects) come from: **Aster** (attack-path analysis), **Yui** (an LLM-driven home assistant), **Koya** (my own CI/CD system) and **Yoji** (my notes, tasks and projects hub, with MCP).

In short, a hybrid profile who can both quit Vim and center a div.
